Rules on the card
  • 0 HP is broken. Negative HP is dead. Broken is a d4: unconscious, a ruined limb or eye, hemorrhage, or dead.
  • A breath restores d4 HP. A night's sleep restores d6. Infection blocks healing and deals d6 each day.
  • Initiative is a d6: 1-3 enemies, 4-6 players. Agility + d6 breaks ties inside the group.
  • Morale is 2d6. Equal or over the morale rating, then d6: 1-3 flee, 4-6 surrender. Check when the leader dies, half the group is gone, or one enemy is down to a third of its HP.
  • Reaction d6: kill, angered, disgusted, neutral, terrified, helpful.
  • Attacking a horde: DR is 13 minus the number of minions. Thirteen or more is an automatic hit. Defending: DR 12, plus 1 for every 5 minions. Horde damage uses the die closest to the number of minions, maximum d12.
  • Optional focus rage: spend the attack. Next attack deals double the rolled damage at -2 DR, and a hit fills one Brutality bar. A hit while you focus breaks it.
  • Dawn of Demise: roll one d6 for every pact used that day. Any double opens a Rift. Three 6s is the Final Rift. Powers replenish. Pacts do not.
